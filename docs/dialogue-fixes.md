# Daily conversations that assume an earlier talk

**Status: all fixed.** Most by a few added words in that day's opening line. Where a reply was simpler to reword, it was
reworded: Mike 11, Grace 22, Hekki 30, Jack 26, Lucia 22, Pirkko 28, and Sam's late wife (Aili) is now named as his wife
wherever he mentions her. Gunnar's 5th, Private Aho's 23rd and Igor's 24th turned out fine on a second look.

Each line below is a conversation where your reply, or the person's opening line, relies on something they said on an
**earlier day** (or that only someone else told you). If you skipped that earlier day, it comes out of nowhere, as with
Tiina's boat and Solveig's London. Both of those are already fixed.

The usual fix is small: add a few words to that day's opening line, so the person says the thing before you can ask about
it. For example, Helmi's 16th would become "...darning one of my sister's jumpers in her lap". Sometimes rewording the reply
is simpler instead.

Found by a scan of every conversation, then checked by hand. **Probably not complete**: the scan finds names and family
words, but it can miss a reply that assumes something without naming it.
