# Quest schedule (19-week cycle)

Rules: each week two factions have quests, with a different pair every week. Each of the seven
factions has 6 quests overall. Week 19 has six factions so the totals add up
(18 weeks x 2 + 6 = 42 = 7 x 6).

## Notes

- No faction appears in two consecutive weeks, except the Communist Party in weeks 18 and 19.
- Gaps between a faction's quests are mostly 3 to 4 weeks (longest is 5).
- Three pairs never share a week: Communists + Kelp Cola, Royal Family + Petroleum,
  Democrats + Church.
- The Crime Syndicate sits out week 19 (an arbitrary choice; any faction could take that role).
