# Quest schedule (the 30 days of September)

The game runs over the 30 days of September. Day 0 is the game intro (from the CIA, before September begins). In the first week
each main faction is introduced on its own day; after that, factions have quests on most days. Days 14, 21 and 30 belong to the
whole island. The data is `QUEST_DAYS`, `QUEST_EVENTS` and `QUEST_TITLES` in `js/data.js`, and the wiki's Quests page shows it as
a table.

Weeks: 1 is days 1-7, 2 is days 8-14, 3 is days 15-21, 4 is days 22-28, and 5 is days 29-30.
