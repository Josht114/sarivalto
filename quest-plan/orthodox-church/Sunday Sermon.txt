Quest: Sunday Sermon
Faction: Orthodox Church
Day: 8 (morning)
Linked with: None
From the plan: Give a sermon
Status: In the game

Plan:
Father Thyme offers it at the monastery on
the morning of 8 September. He can't find
the words for a sermon on the king's
death, and his voice is failing, so he
asks you, a writer, to write it and read
it.
Choose what the island should hear:
Grief: the island mourns its king
together. Orthodox Church +1, Royal Family
+1.
Unity: whoever rules, the island must
stand together. Orthodox Church +1.
Faith over crowns: kings pass away, and
the Church endures. Orthodox Church +1,
Royal Family -1.
Change: the Church must modernise, or be
left behind. Orthodox Church -1.
The congregation gathers in the Monastery
Church: Juniper, Pirkko, Hilda, Olaf,
Augustus and the monks. Step up to the
altar and preach, and see how they take
it.
Then talk to Father Thyme.
It shares day 8 with Black Tide: whichever
you take on first is the day's main quest.
