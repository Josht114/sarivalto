Quest: The Big Rally
Faction: Democratic Party
Day: 28 (morning)
Linked with: None
From the plan: (a new quest for the free
day before the Friendly rivalry)

Plan:
The day before the vote, Erik holds a
rally for free elections in the square in
Kingsgrad, and it has to be the biggest
the island has seen.
Help build the stage and hang the banners,
round up a crowd from the mill, the cafes
and the docks, and keep the Party and the
Syndicate's men from breaking it up.
How big the crowd is, and whether the day
ends in speeches or a brawl, is up to you.
