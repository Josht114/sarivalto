Quest: Black Tide
Faction: Diversified Petroleum
Day: 8 (morning)
Linked with: None
From the plan: Deal with an oil spill
Status: In the game

Plan:
Dario telephones in the morning: he needs
your help, and you're to come to his
office at the HQ.
He tells you a valve has burst at the oil
rig and the sea round it is covered in
oil. On this day the rig's map shows it:
black water with an oily sheen, and oil
splashed over the west of the deck.
Fetch two rig workers, Lars from the crew
quarters and Einar from the deck, and meet
them at the slick by the west rail.
Then choose. At first the only plan is the
pump; looking at the work lamp on the west
rail gives you the idea of setting the
slick alight, and from then on you can
send the workers away for buckets, even
after you have told them to build the
pump.
Clean it up: the workers suggest building
a pump. Bring Einar a hose from the
fishing shop in Kingsgrad, a motor from
the electronics store in Frostholm and a
boom of floats from Harbour Outfitters in
Saltwick. The pump clears the sea.
Diversified Petroleum +1.
Set it on fire: once you have had the
idea, send the workers off to the quarters
for buckets, then knock the work lamp into
the slick so it looks like an accident.
The sea burns, and the west of the deck
stays scorched from then on. Einar,
running back with his bucket, is caught by
the flames: for the rest of the game he
lies bandaged at the doctor's office in
Kingsgrad. Diversified Petroleum -1.
Either way, tell Dario what happened.
