Quest: Beans for Maya
Giver: Erik (Democratic Party leader)
Day: 4 (any time from then)
How you get it: Given during Meet the
Democrats (main quest, day 4)

Plan:
Erik says Maya at the Coffee Roasters in
Eastbrook can't get decent coffee beans
since the harbour master started taxing
imports. Ask Chuck to have some brought
in; they come the next day, and Hekki will
give them to you. Then take them to Maya.
1. Ask Chuck on the big screen to have
some coffee beans brought in
2. Collect the coffee beans from Hekki
(they come the day after you ask Chuck)
3. Take the coffee beans to Maya at the
Coffee Roasters in Eastbrook
