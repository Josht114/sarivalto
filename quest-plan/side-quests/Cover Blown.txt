Quest: Cover Blown
Giver: Solveig (Sub-editor)
Day: 9 (any time from then)
How you get it: Offered by Solveig, at the
Eastbrook Gazette, from day 9

Plan:
Solveig, the Gazette's sub-editor, spent
eleven years on Fleet Street, and works
out that you're no reporter. She confronts
you at her desk. Deal with her one of
three ways:
1. Convince her she's wrong: answer her
three questions about Fleet Street. One
wrong answer and she won't be talked
round, so only the other two ways are left.
2. Pay her off: $500 of your own cash.
3. Kill her: ask her to meet you on the
south jetty at Eastbrook Harbour, and
silence her for good. She's gone for the
rest of the game, and Henrik and Nora
wonder where she is.
No faction points either way.
