Bio:
A self-made American oilman and the CEO of
Diversified Petroleum, who started out
with a pick, a bucket and a hole in the
ground and never forgot it.
He speaks softly and plainly, calls
himself a family man and a friend to the
working people, and wants what is under
the sea around Sarivalto, all of it.
Behind the charm is a man who cannot bear
to see anyone else succeed: he sees the
worst in people, and he says he has no
need to look past it.
He drinks too much, trusts no one, and
keeps a private bowling lane and a
milkshake machine in his office at the HQ,
which he is very proud of.
He lives in a villa in Hope Springs and
would like a government that signs
drilling rights without reading them.
