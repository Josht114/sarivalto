Name: Anna
Gender: Female
Faction: Communist Party
Group: The Union
Partner: None
Job: Communist Party leader
Home: Apartment Block (Saltwick)

Bio:
The leader of Sarivalto's Communist Party,
run from its HQ in Saltwick.
A true believer who grew up gutting
herring on the quay, she wants the island
to join the Soviet Union as a Soviet
republic and belong to the fishermen, mill
hands and miners.
Warm with visitors and ruthless in a
meeting, she calls everyone comrade and
means it.
