Bio:
A CIA agent from Langley, back from leave
on 22 August to a new assignment.
Codename NIGHTJAR. Briefed by Chuck and
flown out by Peggy, arriving at West
Saltwick Harbour on 1 September, with
thirty days on Sarivalto.
The mission, in Chuck's words: an island
that doesn't end up flying a red flag.
Cover: a British reporter, from a London
paper, come to cover the king's death.
Only Hekki, Chuck and George know
otherwise.
Reports to Chuck by day and George by
night, on the big screen or the radio.
A smoker. Starts with $5,000 of CIA funds
and $250 of their own, a notebook, a
camera and three rolls of film, a
codebook, lock picks and a flashlight.
