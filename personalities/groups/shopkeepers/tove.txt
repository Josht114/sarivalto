Name: Tove
Gender: Female
Faction: None
Group: The Shopkeepers
Partner: None
Job: Barber
Home: Apartment Block (Saltwick)

Bio:
The barber in Saltwick, down the path to
the harbour, and the only one on the
island. She shaves fishermen, Party men
and the odd oilman, and keeps every word
they say under the hot towel.
Brisk, dry and fond of the town, which
pays her in fish when it's short.
