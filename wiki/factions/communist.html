Allies of the Soviet Union. Its leader is Anna.

The Communist Party is based in Saltwick, where its headquarters stands, and is led by Anna from her flat in the village's apartment block. Three party members live in the village's apartment block, and two more, Sam and Kate, have their own houses. Across the island in Eastbrook, where most people back the Democrats, Liv and Tor from Eastbrook Harbour are party members too. Its link to Moscow is Yuri, a KGB agent working undercover from a room at Hotel Aurora in Frostholm.
