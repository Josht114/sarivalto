Allies of the Soviet Union.

The Communist Party is based in Saltwick, where its headquarters stands. Three party members live in the village's apartment block, and two more, Sam and Kate, have their own houses. Its link to Moscow is Yuri, a KGB agent working undercover from a room at Hotel Aurora in Frostholm.
