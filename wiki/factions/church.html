The northern orthodox church.

The northern Orthodox Church. Its monastery stands on a mountain top, where the monks live under their abbot, Father Thyme, and his prior, Brother Anselm. Down in Eastbrook it has two faithful members of its own: Pirkko, who runs the food van at the Kelp Cola factory, and Juniper, who owns the Flannel and Folk clothes shop. Its most powerful friend is Prince Augustus, the late king's devout son: the monastery wants him, not his brother Leopold, on the throne.
