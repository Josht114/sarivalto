The travelling circus camped at Lake Ilmari: its ringmaster and performers.

A travelling circus that has pitched its big top and caravans on the shore of Lake Ilmari. Bruno the ringmaster runs it; his performers are Meg the acrobat, Igor the strongman and Buttons the jester, and Madame Zora tells fortunes in her caravan. It is a group, not a faction: it plays its part in the story but cannot win it.
