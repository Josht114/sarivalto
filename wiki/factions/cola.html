A cola company.

Kelp Cola has its headquarters in Kingsgrad's neighbourhood and a factory nearby, joined by the Kelp Cola tram line, which loops from Kingsgrad to the tram stop, the factory and Eastbrook and back. Stand on a platform and press E to call the tram. In Eastbrook it runs the Kelp Cola Museum, a polished history of the company with a gift shop, where Wilma is the curator.
