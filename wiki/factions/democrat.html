A democratic political party, based at the Community Centre in Eastbrook.

The Democratic Party is based at the Community Centre in Eastbrook, the island's second town. Its leader is Erik. Most of Eastbrook backs the party: Maya at the coffee roaster, Jonna at the tattoo shop, Dex with his hot dog cart, Ruby at the record shop, and Bjarne, Knut and Ilse from the lumber mill are all members.
