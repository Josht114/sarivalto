A democratic political party, based at the Community Centre in Eastbrook.

The Democratic Party is based at the Community Centre in Eastbrook, the island's second town. Its leader is Erik. Most of Eastbrook backs the party: the Gazette's editor Henrik and reporter Nora, Maya at the coffee roaster, Jonas at the tattoo shop, Linnea at the Timberline Tavern, Arvid at the Cedar Grill, Pekka with his food van, and Liv and Tor from Eastbrook Harbour are all members.
