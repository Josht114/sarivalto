The Republican Party is based at its headquarters in Frostholm. It has no leader, and the island's two big companies and the Crime Syndicate are each trying to get their own candidate voted in: Harald for Kelp Cola, Magnus for Diversified Petroleum and Carlo for the Syndicate.
