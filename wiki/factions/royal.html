The family of the island's previous king.

The family of the previous king, whose death starts the story. Their seat is the Royal Palace. Not everyone in Democratic Eastbrook wants the monarchy gone: Linnea, the bartender at the Timberline Tavern, and Arvid, the chef at the Cedar Grill, are royalists. The family is divided: Leopold is the heir, but his brother Augustus, a devout churchman, has the backing of the monastery to be crowned instead.
