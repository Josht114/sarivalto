The people who run the island's other businesses: its bars, the jazz club, the cinema, the casino, the arcade, the hotel, the spa, the estate agent and the tattoo parlour.

The people who run the island's businesses that are not shops: the bartenders Ray in Saltwick, Eddie at the City Bar and Linnea at the Timberline Tavern; Sid, who owns The Midnight Note; Otto at the cinema; Vince at the casino, Toni at the arcade and Greta at Hotel Aurora; Saga at the Hot Springs Spa; Sally the estate agent; and Jonas the tattoo artist. It has no leader. It is a group, not a faction: it plays its part in the story but cannot win it.
