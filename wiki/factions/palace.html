The staff of the Royal Palace. Edmund the butler runs the household, with Oskar the footman under him; Marta the cook runs the kitchen and the laundress, Elsa; Ingrid and Lotta are the maids, and Viktor keeps the grounds. Edmund, Oskar and Marta are royalists, loyal to the family they serve. It is a group, not a faction: it plays its part in the story but cannot win it.
