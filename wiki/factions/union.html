The Union

The island's trade union, for the people who work at the Kelp Cola factory, in the fishing trade and at the lumber mill. Its leader is Helen.

The Union is the island's trade union, led by Helen from her flat in Saltwick's apartment block. Most of the workers at the Kelp Cola factory, in the fishing trade and at the lumber mill belong to it: Petra from the cola warehouse, Aino from the factory's bottling line, Bob and Kate from the fish processing plant, the fishermen Tom, Sam and Mike, Gunnar from the harbour warehouse, and Knut and Ilse from the mill. The foremen, Kurt at the factory and Bjarne at the mill, are not members. It is a group, not a faction: it plays its part in the story but cannot win it.
