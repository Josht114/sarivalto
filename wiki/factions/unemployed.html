The Unemployed

TypeGroup (can't win)
BadgeUNE
BasesBeach
PeoplePat, Sirpa, Tiina, Eila, Marjut, Veikko, Hely, Arto, Raili

The island's people without work.

The island's people without work: Pat, a hobo who camps in a tent by the campfire on the beach, Sirpa and Tiina from Saltwick's apartment block, who hang about on the main street by day and sit in the bar in the evenings, Eila and Marjut from the Kingsgrad Apartments, who do the same by the bench at the west end of Kingsgrad and in the City Bar, Veikko and Hely from Eastbrook's Timber Lofts, by the bench in Eastbrook's square and in the Timberline Tavern, and Arto and Raili from Frostholm's Skyline Apartments, by the phone box on the boulevard and in The Midnight Note. It has no leader. It is a group, not a faction: it plays its part in the story but cannot win it.
