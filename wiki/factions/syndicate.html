An organised crime syndicate. Its boss lives in the gated community.

The Crime Syndicate is run by its Don, Gustavo, who lives in a villa in Hope Springs Gated Community and holds court in The Club there. It keeps one of its people working each town: Nico and Mario, bookies in Frostholm and Kingsgrad, Paolo, a bookie in Eastbrook, and Lucia and Paola, enforcers in Saltwick and Hope Springs. Carlo is its candidate for leader of the Republican Party.
