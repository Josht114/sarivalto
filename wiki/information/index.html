Information

Every conversation the player hears goes into the menu's Information tab, under its day, with a one-line summary of what they learned (or, without a summary, what they asked). Some of it changes what people say later: a reply that only appears if you heard a certain conversation (or didn't), or a different opener for someone you've already met. Each conversation is named by who and which day, as in 'Elin:2'; content/daily/README.md says how to write them.
