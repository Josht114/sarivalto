Controls

Every key in the game. Everything can also be done with the mouse: click the buttons on screen. On a phone or tablet, the arrow pad and the E button under the game do the walking and the talking.

Getting about

Arrows or W A S DWalk. Walk off a labelled edge of the map, onto a door, or onto a boat berth to go somewhere else.
E or Space or EnterTalk to the person you're facing, or use what's in front of you: a bed, the telephone, the radio set (tune it in to call Langley), a tram stop, a shop counter, a game. The hint above you says what it will do.
PPick up a piece of furniture you put down in a home of your own.

Conversations

Up or Down or W or SChoose a reply.
1 to 9Pick that reply straight away.
E or Space or EnterSay the reply you've chosen.
EscapeEnd the conversation.

The menu

The menu has the Inventory, Quests, Journal, Map, Save and Options tabs (the Map tab only once you have a Map of Sarivalto: the newsstands and the bookshop sell them). The game waits while it's open.

MOpen or close the menu.
Tab or Shift+Tab, 1 to 6Change tab.
Left or Right or A or DChange tab (except on the Inventory tab).
Arrows or W A S DOn the Inventory tab, move through what you're carrying.
E or Space or EnterOn the Inventory tab, do the first thing the chosen item can do.
QQuick save, any time you're not talking or travelling.

The Options tab has the volume, and Quit to the title screen, which asks whether to save first (in the quick save).

Closing things

E or Space or Enter or EscapeClose the message box in the middle of the screen (or click Continue). Messages and quest news wait there until you close it, and the game waits too. (Bumping into things, such as stairs that go the other way or a shut door, gives a short note along the bottom of the screen that fades on its own.)
EscapeClose the one thing on top: the menu, a conversation, a list of choices or a game.
ZClose everything that's open at once: the menu, a conversation, a list of choices, the message box, and furniture you're placing.

Lists of choices

Such as the tram's stops, a vending machine or a card table.

Up or Down or W or SChoose.
EPick it.
EscapeClose the list.

Shops and the wardrobe

Arrows or W A S DIn a shop, move through the stock.
CBuy the chosen item with CIA funds.
PBuy it with your own money.
Up or Down or W or SIn the wardrobe (and the Cover Story Department), choose what to change.
Left or Right or A or DIn the wardrobe, change it.

Furniture in your home

Putting down something you've bought for a home of your own.

Arrows or W A S DMove it.
Enter or E or SpacePut it down.
EscapeKeep it for later.

Games

Escape leaves any of them.

Space or E or EnterPicking a lock: lift the pin while the needle is in the green.
Left or Right, Space or E or EnterCracking a safe: turn the dial (hold Shift for five at a time), and set the wheel when you hear the click.
Space or E or EnterForging a card: write each line while the needle is in the green.
Left or RightTuning the radio: turn the dial (hold Shift to turn it faster) until the static clears, and hold it there.
Space or E or EnterFishing: cast, strike when something bites, and hold to reel in.
Space or E or EnterSlot machine: pull the lever.
Left or Right, Space or E or Enter, H or S or DBlackjack: change your bet, deal; then hit, stand or double.
Space or E or Enter, C or R or XPoker: deal; then call, raise or fold.
Arrows or W A S D, SpaceArcade: steer the snake; move the paddle; move the gun, and fire.
Arrows or W A S D, Enter or SpaceTattoo parlour: up and down choose the design or where it goes, left and right change it; buy it.
Space or PAt the cinema: pause the film.

The title screen and the opening film

Arrows or W A S DOn the title screen, choose Continue, New game or Load game.
E or Space or EnterPick it.
Escape or Space or E or EnterSkip the opening film.

Other keys

FFull screen, on and off.
NDay or night (for testing).

Under the game

The buttons under the game screen: Jump to (go straight to any place), Date (go to any day), Day or Night, Menu, Music and Radio (on and off), and Full screen. The dashed Test box adds or takes away a faction's points, moves on to the next day, and sets the volume (the same volume as on the Options tab).
