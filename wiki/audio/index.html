Audio

The background sound each place has, made in the browser as you play. Places without any are quiet. Radio Sarivalto, when it is switched on (the Radio button, or Listen on a transistor radio), plays instead of all of it, anywhere. The Music button turns the music and place sounds off and on, and the volume is set on the Options tab of the game's menu.

A small choir of men's voices sings the Salve Regina, one line in the Dorian mode, over a low hummed drone, in a long stone-church echo.

Jazz

A quartet swings through a medium-tempo tune in F: a walking bass, the ride cymbal and brushes, piano chords on the off-beats, and now and then a breathy tenor sax taking a solo, in a small club's echo.

Plays at: The Midnight Note

Royal processional

A stately Baroque processional in D major: strings holding the chords over a walking bass, a harpsichord rippling through them, timpani, and two trumpets playing the tune in the dotted rhythms of a grand entrance, in a palace hall's long echo.

Romani music

A travelling band's csárdás in D harmonic minor: slow and mournful at first, a violin sliding into its notes over accordion chords and a rolling cimbalom, then breaking into a fast dance with an oom-pah bass, and back again.

Plays at: Big Top, Ringmaster's Caravan, Performers' Caravan, Fortune Teller's Caravan

Bottling line

Casino swing

Coffee shop

People talking at the tables, the espresso machine and its steam wand frothing milk, the grinder, cups on saucers and spoons stirring, the beans popping in the roaster, and an acoustic guitar in the background.

Ominous

Dark Cold War thriller music: a low drone that never settles, a slow heartbeat pulse, uneasy string chords swelling and fading, lone high piano notes in a long echo and now and then a low brass swell.

The woods

The snowy woods along the roads and in the Pine Forest: a light breeze in the pines, a branch creaking, snow slumping off one; by day birds singing, a crow far off and a woodpecker drumming, and by night an owl.

Fife and drum

A fife and drum corps of the 1860s plays a march across the parade ground: two fifes in thirds over a snare drum's accents, flams and rolls, and a bass drum on every beat.

The sea
