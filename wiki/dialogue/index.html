Everyone on the island has their own conversation for each day of September they can be reached (content/daily/, bundled into js/daily.js). They were written by two Claude models: the first round by Claude Opus, and the rest, after the first round was cut short, by Claude Sonnet, which costs less but writes plainer conversations, with fewer branches. The Sonnet days are the ones to polish first.
