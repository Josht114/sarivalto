Quests

The game runs over the 30 days of September. It opens on day 0 (22 August, at CIA Headquarters) with the CIA's game intro; over the first week each of the seven main factions is introduced on its own day, and from then on two factions (or more) have quests on most days. Three days belong to the whole island: Event 1 on day 14, the volcano on day 21 and the finale on day 30. The intro quests made so far are named in the schedule; the quests in the game so far are described below it.

There are two kinds of quest. A main quest belongs to a day: its giver offers it that morning (a gold star), you can take on only one main quest a day, and you can't sit and wait for the evening until it's done. A side quest (a silver star, or given to you during another quest) can be taken on and done at any time. Chuck offers his on the morning video call, on the big screen in the Hidden Radio Room; George, at Langley, takes the evening calls.

Main quests

22 August

Prepare Your Alias Main quest

FromDoris
How you get itYours from the start of a new game

Before you go anywhere, you need a cover. Go to the Cover Story Department, off the lobby at CIA Headquarters, and have Doris make you up: choose how you look and what you wear.

  1. Choose your look and clothes in the Cover Story Department, off the lobby (gives the main quest Your Briefing)

Your Briefing Main quest

FromChuck
How you get itGiven when Prepare Your Alias is done

Chuck is waiting for you in the Langley Office, up the stairs from the lobby. Go and get your briefing.

  1. Get your briefing from Chuck, in the Langley Office upstairs

1 September

Meet the Communists Main quest

FromChuck
How you get itOffered on the morning video call on 1 September

Report in to Chuck on the radio set in the Hidden Radio Room. Then Chuck wants a read on the Communist Party, who want the island to join the Soviet Union: go to their HQ in Saltwick and have a conversation with Anna, the Party leader.

  1. Report in to Chuck on the radio in the Hidden Radio Room
  2. Have a conversation with Anna, the Communist Party leader
  3. Report back to Chuck on the big screen in the Hidden Radio Room

Meet the Syndicate Main quest

FromA stranger on the telephone

A stranger telephoned this morning: a friend would like to meet you in the back room of the pizzeria in Kingsgrad. Go, sit in at the card table, and play a few hands with whoever is waiting there. Then report back to Chuck.

Beans for Maya Side quest

FromErik
How you get itGiven during Meet the Democrats

Erik says Maya at the Coffee Roasters in Eastbrook can't get decent coffee beans since the harbour master started taxing imports. Ask Chuck to have some brought in; they come the next day, and Hekki will give them to you. Then take them to Maya.
