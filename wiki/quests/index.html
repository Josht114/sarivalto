Quests

The game runs over the 30 days of September. It opens on day 0 with the CIA's game intro; over the first week each of the seven main factions is introduced on its own day, and from then on two factions (or more) have quests on most days. Three days belong to the whole island: Event 1 on day 14, the volcano on day 21 and the finale on day 30.
