Quests

The game runs over the 30 days of September. It opens on day 0 (22 August, at CIA Headquarters) with the CIA's game intro; over the first week each of the seven main factions is introduced on its own day, and from then on two factions (or more) have quests on most mornings, and from the second week, every three or four evenings, George at Langley has a job for the CIA. Three days belong to the whole island: Event 1 on day 14, the volcano on day 21 and the finale on day 30. The intro quests made so far are named in the schedule; the quests in the game so far are described below it.

There are two kinds of quest. A main quest belongs to a day: its giver offers it that morning (a gold star), you can take on only one main quest a day, and you can't sit and wait for the evening until it's done. A side quest (a silver star, or given to you during another quest) can be taken on and done at any time. Chuck offers his on the morning video call, on the big screen in the Hidden Radio Room; George, at Langley, takes the evening calls. The side quests are listed on the day they can first be had. George's evening quests are marked with a moon (☾). A tick (✓) marks the quests that are in the game already.

Black Tide Main quest

FromDario
How you get itA telephone call on the morning of 8 September

Dario telephoned: something has gone wrong at the oil rig, and he wants your help. Go to his office at Diversified Petroleum HQ in Frostholm.

Endings

  • Build the pump: Diversified Petroleum +1 point
  • Set the fire: Diversified Petroleum -1 point
  1. Go to Dario's office at Diversified Petroleum HQ
  2. Fetch Lars from the crew quarters on the oil rig
  3. Fetch Einar from the deck of the oil rig
  4. Go to the slick at the west rail of the rig, and decide what to do with Lars and Einar
  5. Bring Einar a hose (the fishing shop, Kingsgrad), a motor (the electronics store, Frostholm) and a boom of floats (Harbour Outfitters, Saltwick) (only if you choose to build the pump; +1 point for Diversified Petroleum; hand over hose, small motor, boom of floats)
  6. Knock the work lamp on the west rail into the slick, as if by accident (only if you choose to set the fire; -1 point for Diversified Petroleum)
  7. Tell Dario what happened at the rig (only if you choose to set the fire)
  8. Tell Dario the spill is cleaned up (only if you choose to build the pump)

The People's Cola Main quest

FromHelen
How you get itOffered in person on 9 September

Helen wants a cola of the Party's own, made by the workers for the workers, to break Kelp Cola's hold on the island. Find an idea for the flavour (the island's cooks and vendors know what people like: Rosa at the hot dog cart outside Kelp Cola HQ, say), then brew it with Kelp Cola's own equipment, in the research lab under their HQ in Kingsgrad.

Endings

  • Make it for the Party: Communist Party +1 point
  • Sell the recipe to Phil: Communist Party -1 point, Kelp Cola +1 point
  1. Ask around for an idea for the flavour (the island's cooks and vendors: Rosa at the hot dog cart outside Kelp Cola HQ, say)
  2. Scoop up some lava from the pool in the Lava Tube, through the cave mouth on the volcano's lower ledge (you get the tin of lava)
  3. Get Noora, the lab technician, out of the research lab under Kelp Cola HQ
  4. Brew the People's Cola at a lab bench in the research lab (you get the the people's cola)
  5. Deal with Phil, who has caught you in his lab
  6. Take the People's Cola to Helen at the Communist Party HQ in Saltwick (only if you choose to make it for the Party; +1 point for Communist Party)
  7. Go back to Helen at the Communist Party HQ in Saltwick (only if you choose to sell the recipe to Phil; -1 point for Communist Party; +1 point for Kelp Cola)

Lights Out Side quest

FromGeorge
How you get itOffered from 1 September

George wants the lighthouse at Eastbrook Harbour dark tonight. Get Signe, the keeper, out of the lighthouse, climb to the lamp room and smash the lamp.

Endings

  • Take Signe for a drink: no change to the factions’ points
  • Sink her boat: no change to the factions’ points
  1. Get Signe out of the lighthouse: talk her into a night off, in the keeper's room at the foot of the tower
  2. Drill a hole in Signe's boat, moored at the jetty (a hand drill: the supermarket, open late, or Harbour Outfitters in Saltwick by day) (only if you choose to sink her boat)
  3. Tell Signe her boat is sinking (only if you choose to sink her boat)
  4. Climb the spiral stair to the lamp room and smash the lamp
  5. Report to George on the big screen in your room
