Saltwick, the island's fishing village, is home to the Communist Party's headquarters, a fish processing plant, a fish shop, a tired newsstand on the main street, a bar, an apartment block and houses for the fishermen and fishmongers. The village is run down: peeling paint, boarded-up windows, patched roofs, broken cobbles, slush and junk.
