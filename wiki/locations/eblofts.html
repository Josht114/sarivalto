The Timber Lofts, Eastbrook's apartment block among the fir trees: four floors of five brick-walled flats with wooden floors, bookcases and record shelves, every one of them taken. Maya, Jonas, Erik, Linnea, Arvid, Liv and Tor live here, with Dex from the hot dog cart, Wilma from the Kelp Cola Museum, Juniper from the clothes shop, Rufus from the record shop, the mill crew Bjarne, Knut and Ilse, the Crime Syndicate's Paolo, and Jarmo, who spends his days inside Kelpy the cup.
