The Timber Lofts, Eastbrook's apartment block among the fir trees: brick-walled flats with wooden floors, bookcases and record shelves. Maya, Jonas, Erik, Linnea, Arvid, Liv and Tor live here. It has three floors of five flats each, and some of the Crime Syndicate's people live here too.
