CIA Headquarters at Langley, Virginia, where the story begins, far from the island and reached only from the Jump to list. Its front doors lead out to the airstrip, where a little seaplane flies you out to the island. The Agency's seal is set into the granite floor of the lobby; on the wall above, the Memorial Wall's rows of carved stars stand between two flags, with the Book of Honor lying open in its case beneath them. A statue stands to one side, and stairs beside the security desk lead up to the offices.
