Rosaria's pizzeria in Kingsgrad: a wood-fired oven, a counter, a shelf of wine and red-checked tables in front. Behind a door at the back is a shady back room with a card table, a safe, crates and a desk of ledgers: the pizzeria is a front for the Crime Syndicate, and this is where it counts its money. The Syndicate plays Texas hold'em at the card table in the back room, for $1 and $2 blinds, and will deal you in. Beside the counter a door leads through to the kitchen, where the dough is made.
