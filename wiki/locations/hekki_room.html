Hidden Radio Room

TypeInterior
DistrictSouth West
InsideHekki's Shack (Radio Tower)
Size22 × 9 tiles

A secret room behind a bookcase in Hekki's shack, packed with radio equipment, tape recorders and banks of monitor screens. Hekki uses it to listen in on the island. Through a doorway in a partition at the back is where you, the CIA agent, live, hidden from the island, and where the game starts: a bed, a stove, a table, and shelves of supplies and tinned food. A tear-off calendar above the bed shows the date: the game runs through the 30 days of September, and sleeping in the bed moves on to the next morning. Maps of the island hang on the walls; look at one to see the whole island and where you are. On the back wall hangs a big screen with a camera on top, for video calls to CIA headquarters. In the living space a tall wardrobe holds your clothes. On the desktop PC in the radio room runs the Faction Tracker, which keeps score of how each faction stands.
