Frostholm, the corporate city in the north of the island, next door to Diversified Petroleum's headquarters. Glass towers and neon line its paved streets: the Petro Tower, Hotel Aurora, the Skyline Apartments, Nordbank, the Republican Party HQ, a casino, an electronics store, an arcade, the Nordmart store and Polar Burger, a fast-food restaurant, under billboards for Kelp Cola, Diversified Petroleum and Nordmart. Its harbour is North Frostholm Harbour. The Midnight Note, a jazz club with a LIVE JAZZ marquee, stands beside the arcade.
