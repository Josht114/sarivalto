Hope Springs Gated Community, reached from Kingsgrad along South Road. The island's richest live here: Gustavo, the Crime Syndicate's Don, and Phil and Dario, who run Kelp Cola and Diversified Petroleum, each have a villa. It is immaculate: swept flagstone paths behind gate pillars, clipped hedges, ornate lamps, flower beds, topiary, statues and a fountain between the villas, formal gardens either side, and the Hope Springs Residences, an apartment block for the wealthy. It has paths to South Private Harbour and the Private Beach.
