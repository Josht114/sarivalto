The island's volcano, reached from the Mountains to the east. It rises in terraces: from the lower slopes, stairs cut into the cliff on the west side climb to a middle ledge, and a second stair at its east end climbs to the summit, where a rim runs round the smoking lava crater. Steam vents hiss on every level. On the lower slopes, a path leads down from the main path to the Hot Springs Spa, a timber bathhouse with two steaming outdoor pools fed by the volcano's springs: a big hot pool and a small plunge pool, with lanterns and benches round them. It is a dead end.
