The island's volcano, reached from the Mountains to the east. It rises in terraces: from the lower slopes, stairs cut into the cliff on the west side climb to a middle ledge, and a second stair at its east end climbs to the summit, where a rim runs round the smoking lava crater. Steam vents hiss on every level. It is a dead end.
