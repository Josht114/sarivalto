Inside Kelp Cola's headquarters: a reception desk, a fridge stocked with the company's cola, offices, and the boardroom table where Phil holds meetings. The walls are hung with old Kelp Cola adverts (bottles, Kelpy the cup, sunbursts and seaside scenes), and glass cases by the back wall and in the lobby show off the company's bottles through the years, beside a stack of Kelp Cola crates. Stairs at the back lead up to Phil's own office, which has a couple of the old adverts too. Harald, a Kelp Cola executive and the company's candidate for leader of the Republican Party, works at the desk in the corner office.
