The Royal Palace, home of the previous king's family. It is reached from Kingsgrad. In front of it is a formal garden: an avenue of clipped hedges between two frozen fountains, with statues, benches and flower beds. Everything else, from the chapel to the servants' quarters, is inside the palace; the servants use their own door at the left-hand end.
