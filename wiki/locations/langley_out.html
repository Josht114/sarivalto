The airstrip outside CIA Headquarters at Langley, on a summer lawn: a path from the front doors down to a tarmac runway, where a little white seaplane on wheeled floats waits with Peggy, its pilot. Once Chuck has briefed you, it flies you out to Sarivalto; a fishing boat meets it at sea at dawn and brings you into West Saltwick Harbour on 1 September.
