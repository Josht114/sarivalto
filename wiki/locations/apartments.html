The village's apartment block: three floors of five flats each, joined by stairs from the corridor. The communists Anna, Mike and Helen live here, along with Kaisa the radio DJ, Olga from the fish shop and Gunnar from the harbour warehouse, Sirpa and Tiina, who are out of work, and the Crime Syndicate's Lucia.
