The harbour of Eastbrook: a timber customs house and a warehouse on the quay, a long pier out to the boat berths, a jetty where the fishing boats tie up, and, on a rocky spit to the north-east at the end of the pier, a red-and-white lighthouse whose beam sweeps the water at night. Boats run to West Saltwick Harbour, North Frostholm Harbour and the oil rig.
