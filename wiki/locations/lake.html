Lake Ilmari, a quiet lake in the pines west of North West Road. The Valto River flows in from the east and out to the south, on its way to the beach. There is a wooden jetty with rowboats, and a path south to the beach. A travelling circus has pitched its big top and three painted caravans on the east shore: Bruno the ringmaster greets visitors at the big top, and Pippo the clown entertains by the campfire.
