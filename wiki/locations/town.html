Kingsgrad is the island's main city and its hub: roads run from here to the north, east, south and west, and it also leads to the Royal Palace, Kelp Cola HQ and South West Road, which leads to the Army Barracks and Hope Springs Gated Community. In the city are a gun shop, a supermarket, a bar, a tailor, a casino, a book shop, a fishing shop, an estate agent, streets of houses (one of them for sale), a way down into the sewers, and the terminus of the Kelp Cola tram line. The Democratic Party's office is here.
