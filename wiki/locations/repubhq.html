The Republican Party's headquarters in Frostholm, where the party is choosing a new leader. Margit, the party secretary, runs the election from the podium beside the ballot box. Each candidate has a campaign desk: Harald for Kelp Cola, Magnus for Diversified Petroleum and Carlo for the Crime Syndicate.
