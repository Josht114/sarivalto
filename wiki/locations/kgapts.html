Kingsgrad Apartments, an ordinary city block behind the Civil Service Bureau: four floors of five flats each. Toni, Otto, Sergeant Hanna, Doctor Lind, Rosa, Ville, Bertil, Elin, Kurt and Petra live here, and so do the Crime Syndicate's Mario, Silvio, Fabio, Renzo, Piero, Angelo, Beppe, Ugo and Elio.
