Aili's Diner

TypeInterior
DistrictSouth West
InsideSaltwick
Size16 × 11 tiles
PeopleAili

Aili's Diner, in the south-east corner of Saltwick: a run-down diner, open day and night, with a crooked neon sign missing half its letters. Behind the long counter are a grill, a fryer, a pie case and a coffee urn; out front, three cracked booths and a jukebox. Aili sells coffee, hot dogs, burgers and a plate of whatever's on, cheaper than anywhere else on the island.
