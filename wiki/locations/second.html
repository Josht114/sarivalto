Eastbrook, the island's second town. It has its own harbour, Eastbrook Harbour, and a stop on the Kelp Cola tram line, from which the tram runs back to Kingsgrad. It has a rustic, creative feel: timber houses with porches and string lights, brick streets, fir trees, bicycles, and hand-painted signs. On the main street are the Community Centre, the office of the town's newspaper, the Eastbrook Gazette, a coffee roaster and a tattoo shop, with the Cedar Grill restaurant and the Timberline Tavern at either end of the street. Fir forest grows up to the town on both sides, and the Timber Lofts apartment block stands among the trees to the east.
