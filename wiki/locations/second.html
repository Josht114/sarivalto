Eastbrook, the island's second town: an old timber town that has gone hip. It has its own harbour, Eastbrook Harbour, a stop on the Kelp Cola tram line, and a track that leads west to the Mill, the lumber mill, and on to the Pine Forest. Its brick streets are lined with timber houses painted mustard, slate blue, teal and oxblood, with porches, window boxes, string lights and hand-painted signs full of puns. Upper Street has the Timber Lofts apartment block, Pekka's house, the Kelp Cola Museum and its gift shop, Henrik's and Nora's houses and the Community Centre. On the main street are the Cedar Grill, the Slow Drip coffee roasters, Vinyl Resting Place, a record shop with a mural painted across its front, the Ink and Pine tattoo shop, Flannel and Folk, a vintage clothes shop, the Eastbrook Gazette and the Timberline Tavern. Between the main street and Tram Street are a brick-paved food-cart plaza, where Dex runs a hot dog cart, and a little park with a KEEP EASTBROOK WEIRD sign. Alder Lane runs down the middle of town to the tram platform.
