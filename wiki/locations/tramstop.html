Tram Stop

TypeRoad
DistrictSouth East
Faction baseKelp Cola
Size22 × 14 tiles
Walk toKelp Cola HQ, Kelp Cola Factory, Pine Forest

A stop on the Kelp Cola tram line, which runs in a loop from Kingsgrad past the company's headquarters to this stop, the cola factory and Eastbrook, then back to Kingsgrad along South East Road. Here there is a platform and shelter beside the track, under the overhead wire. A trail leads south into the Pine Forest.
