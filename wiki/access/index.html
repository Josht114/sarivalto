Access

The island doesn't open all at once. The story begins on 22 August at CIA Headquarters in Langley, before you reach the island at all. From 1 September the island opens up a part at a time, as the snow is cleared; once a place is open it stays open for the rest of the month.

Day by day

The island on each day it changes: open places in their district's colour, the ones that open that day outlined in gold, closed ones grey, and a ring on each blocked road.

In the game

  • Snow banks. A road from an open place into a closed one has a deep bank of snow across it, one step in from the edge, and the way out is labelled (closed). Walking into the snow says so.
  • The sign. Beside every snow bank stands a red and white DANGER / SNOW sign. Read it (E) and it says: DANGER! SNOW. NO ACCESS. and names the place the road leads to.
  • Boats and trams. Boats don't run to a harbour that is still snowed in, and the tram only offers the stops that are open.
  • The map. On the island map in the game (the menu's Map tab, or the map on the wall at home), the places not open yet are shaded, and a snow bank marks each blocked road.
  • Getting there. Moving from 22 August into September takes you from Langley to your home on the island; going back to 22 August takes you back to Langley. The Jump to test list still goes anywhere.

Changing it

The days are set in js/data.js: OPEN_ON gives each district its day, and OPEN_AT gives a single place a day of its own. The map shapes the game shades come from tools/build-map.js, which writes js/mapgeo.js.
